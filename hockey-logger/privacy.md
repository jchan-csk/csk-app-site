# Hockey Logger privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · Published by CSK International Inc.

Hockey Logger is an iPhone app for a parent to log their own child's hockey games from the stands. This policy explains what the app records and where it goes.

## The short version

- **We don't collect anything.** Hockey Logger has no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising or tracking. CSK International never receives your data.
- **Everything stays on your phone.** Game logs, audio, voice notes, video and photos are stored in the app on your iPhone.
- **Optional iCloud sync goes to your own iCloud.** If you turn on iCloud, game logs, teams and rosters are copied to your private iCloud account so your other devices can see them. Audio, video and photos are never synced.
- **You share only what you choose.** Exports (CSV, spreadsheet, transcript) leave the app only when you share them yourself.

## What the app stores, and why

| What | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player and team details: name, jersey numbers, teams, roles, rosters (teammates' numbers and last names) | To set up games and show stats | On your phone; in your iCloud if sync is on |
| Game logs: shifts, faceoffs, events, times, scores, notes | The purpose of the app | On your phone; in your iCloud if sync is on |
| Game audio and voice notes | To keep a timeline of the game and turn your notes into text | On your phone only |
| Voice-note text | So notes can be read and exported | On your phone; in your iCloud if sync is on |
| Game video you import (for example from LiveBarn) | To review and log the game from video | On your phone only, excluded from device backups |
| Warmup photos | To help find your player on video | On your phone only |
| App settings | To remember your preferences | On your phone |

## Permissions the app asks for

Each permission is optional; the app works without it, minus that feature.

- **Microphone:** to record game audio and voice notes.
- **Speech recognition:** to turn voice notes into text. Recognition runs on the iPhone; the app requires on-device recognition, so audio is not sent to Apple for this.
- **Camera and Photos:** to take or pick warmup photos and pick game video. The app only sees the items you choose.
- **Calendar:** to read upcoming events and fill in game details. The app never changes your calendar.

## Children

Hockey Logger is meant to be used by a parent or guardian, not by children. It records information about a child (their games, and their name and number if entered) only on the parent's own devices and, if sync is on, in the parent's own iCloud. CSK International does not collect, see or store any of it.

## iCloud

iCloud sync uses Apple's CloudKit private database, which only your Apple ID can access. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Deleting your data

- **One game:** in Games, swipe left on the game and tap Delete. This removes the log, audio, video and photos from the phone, and the log from iCloud.
- **Everything on the phone:** delete the app.
- **Everything in iCloud:** on iPhone, Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Hockey Logger › Delete Data.
